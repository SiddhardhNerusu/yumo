# Yumo — changelog

## 1.0 build 2026-10-19 evening (one search engine)
- **One scorer ranks every list** — Add food (Your foods · Generic · Chains · Scanned · Branded), I prepped…, Staples, and Open Food Facts results — built on the standard retrieval recipe. Coverage first: a row that names more of the typed words always ranks above one that names fewer, and a row must name at least half of them to show (so "tempura chicken chunks" by a brand called Rooster never answers "red rooster potato albert"). Then each word scores match quality (exact 1.0 · prefix 0.85, 0.6 while a 1–2 letter word is still being typed · fuzzy 0.5 — same first letter, one edit up to 7 letters) × rarity (log(1 + N / df), so "rooster" outweighs "potato") × position (the first word is the head noun, 1.5×); brand words count the same as name words. Bonuses: the whole line as a name prefix +2, contained +1, your history log(1 + useCount) × 1.2, raw rows +0.4, −0.02 per character of name so "Potatoes" beats "Sweet Potato Fries - Albert Bartlett" on a tie. Plurals are stemmed ("potatoes" = "potato"). 1–3 ms per query over the 4,160-row catalogue, off the main thread.
- Typed brand / variety words stay in the query next to their nutritional fallback ("rooster" → rooster, potato, red): the catalogue matches the fallback, a packaged product matches the word, both through the same rule. Words no catalogue row carries make the **Branded · Open Food Facts group lead** (in Add food and I prepped…) and trigger a second lookup on those words alone.
- Add food: live Open Food Facts search now runs automatically whenever the line names a brand or a word the catalogue can't cover (before: only on a known brand token or the "Show branded" tap).
- Verified on the simulator: "red rooster potato albert" → Potatoes · Albert Bartlett (both bags), Irish Rooster Potatoes · Tesco, then their sweet potatoes and fries, then every catalogue red-potato row.

## 1.0 build 2026-10-19 pm (device feedback — search lag, staples, the planner's empty states)
- **Typing lag fixed.** The fuzzy matcher used to re-fetch all 4,160 foods and re-split every name on every keystroke, on the main thread. Search now runs on an in-memory `FoodIndex` (built once per launch, rebuilt when the food count changes) in a background task with a 120 ms debounce — I prepped… search and the Staples editor both.
- **I prepped… entry always shows on today**, staples or not; with none, the sheet leads to "Add your staples first". Before, the sparkle was hidden until you had staples or history, so new users never learned it existed.
- **Weigh-search rows show the variant** ("canned, drained solids", "old, flesh only · raw") instead of "from the catalogue"; two rows no longer read as duplicates. Raw rows rank above canned / frozen / fried for the same words.
- **UK potato varieties**: Maris Piper, King Edward, Desiree, Rooster, Estima, Wilja → the CoFID "old" rows; Charlotte, Jersey Royals, baby → "new". There is no per-variety nutrition in any public table; this is what every UK database does.
- **Scan a barcode into your staples**: the barcode button in the Staples search field opens the scanner (Open Food Facts → USDA Branded) with "Add as a staple".
- **I prepped… step 1 is the staples editor too**: a catalogue row's + now keeps the food as a staple (shown in its class group, weight field unfolds), the "Add your staples first" card is plain text with no page behind it, and a barcode button sits right of the search field. Searches of 3+ letters also query Open Food Facts by name — a "Packaged · Open Food Facts" group, one row per barcode, + saves it locally and keeps it as a staple. Every food reachable, one source of truth per product.
- **Catalogue search is MFP-style now**: a row shows when any word matches and the list is ordered by how many words matched, so "potato red ro" keeps showing every potato while you type instead of going blank. When the catalogue can't cover a word (a variety, a brand) the packaged group leads.
- **Open Food Facts name search moved to search.openfoodfacts.org** — the legacy endpoint answers 503, which is why no packaged product ever appeared. "rooster potatoes" now returns the Tesco / SuperValu / O'Shea Rooster bags with nutrition; products without nutrition are left out, never shown with guessed numbers.
- Packaged lookup is UK-first (products sold in the UK, English names), waits for a complete word and a 350 ms pause, keeps only products that name all or all-but-one of the typed words, and leads the list only when the catalogue can't cover a word. No worldwide noise for a half-typed "ro".
- Packaged name-search rows must pass an Atwater check (4P + 4C + 9F within 30 % of the stated kcal); a crowd-sourced "79 kcal, 1 g carbs" potato is dropped rather than shown. Scanned barcodes keep their own flow.
- Packaged relevance: a product must name one of the words the catalogue itself can't cover ("albert", "rooster"), ranked by how many typed words it names — so "potato albert red rooster" shows Albert Bartlett's bags and the Rooster bags above the catalogue's red-potato rows. A scanned staple that isn't on Open Food Facts offers "Add it from the label" (the Create-food form with the barcode filled in).
- When the catalogue can't cover part of the query, a second Open Food Facts lookup runs on just those words ("albert rooster") and merges by barcode; packaged rows rank by words named, then uncovered words named, then the shorter name — "Potatoes · Albert Bartlett" above their sweet potatoes and fries.
- **Matcher fallback**: when no row matches every word ("potato albert", a brand the tables don't carry), the words that match nothing are dropped and the rest searched again, so you see every potato row instead of nothing. "rooster" → the red-potato rows.
- **Short day from the planner** now shows an "Add a carb or fat staple ›" card that opens the Staples editor, instead of only a red status tag. The planner still only sizes staples — it never invents foods.
